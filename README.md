# Viva Booth
Students practise a thesis defence or class presentation out loud.
They can paste their paper first (title, research question, abstract, up to five references). We read that
and look those citations up on Scholarxiv before they speak. Then they talk through Voxide. When they
stop, we:
coach how to say it (keep one thing, fix two, give one replacement line);
check the talk against their manuscript;
mark each citation in Scholarxiv / not found / unverified (we never invent a paper);
ask two viva questions from papers we actually found (or from their abstract if none hit).
They can do a second take.
If they paste nothing, open talk still works: we pull citations from the speech only, then the same
Scholarxiv check.
We do not decide if science is “true.” We decide: did they say what their paper says, does the named
source exist, and does their sentence match the abstract we fetched.
Voice is the product. Host on EthioDeploy. No payments.
